# BelowTrace resident pilot

## Purpose

Run a small, supervised usability pilot before describing BelowTrace as a service or making impact claims. The question is practical: can a Detroit resident facing a basement backup understand the next safe action, preserve the right details, and find the relevant public program without the app pretending to diagnose a pipe or decide a claim?

## Scope

| Item | Proposal |
| --- | --- |
| Partner | One Detroit community organization that already helps residents with water, flooding, or home repair |
| Participants | 8–12 Detroit adults; include homeowners and renters if the partner serves both |
| Format | 30-minute moderated session: 10-minute task walk-through, 10-minute interview, 10-minute debrief |
| Data | Participants use a sample address by default. A real address is optional and remains in that participant's browser. Do not collect case facts, photos, account numbers, or service-request numbers. |
| Outcome | A prioritized list of user failures and a short, anonymized findings memo. It is not a legal, engineering, or program-eligibility study. |

## Tasks to observe

1. Start a backup case and find the immediate safety and DWSD reporting guidance.
2. Record a service-request number and explain what the app knows versus what DWSD or a plumber must confirm.
3. Locate the PSRP screening guide and name the documents it requests.
4. Correct an answer and confirm the resident understands that the program makes the final decision.

## Success thresholds

Do not claim impact from a pilot until the raw notes support it. The initial decision bar is:

- At least 8 of 10 participants can find the immediate reporting action without coaching.
- At least 8 of 10 can say that a nearby public main and rain do **not** establish responsibility or a claim outcome.
- At least 7 of 10 can find the relevant program's official application route.
- No participant believes BelowTrace filed a report, contacted DWSD, or submitted a claim for them.

If a threshold fails, fix that flow and re-test it. Report participant count, recruitment channel, task completion and major limitations; never report a percentage as citywide impact.

## Safeguards

- Participation is voluntary. The partner controls recruitment and can end a session at any time.
- Obtain plain-language consent before recording notes. Do not record audio, video, addresses, account numbers or case documents for this first pilot.
- The facilitator should say: “BelowTrace is a navigation tool, not a utility locate, engineering inspection, legal representative, or City decision-maker.”
- If a participant has an active emergency, pause the session and direct them to the official emergency/reporting channel shown in the product. Do not treat their incident as research.

## Deliverables before recruiting

1. Share the one-page partner brief in `partner-brief.md`.
2. Agree on recruitment wording and any participant thank-you budget.
3. Have the partner review the session guide in `session-guide.md`.
4. Test the live URL on a phone, including the language and accessibility needs of the recruited group.
