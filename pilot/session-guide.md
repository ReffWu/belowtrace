# Facilitator guide — 30-minute resident pilot

## Opening (2 minutes)

“Thanks for trying an early version of BelowTrace. We are testing the website, not you. It does not report a problem, submit a claim, find a private pipe, or decide who is responsible. You can stop at any time and skip any question. Please use the sample address unless you prefer your own; we will not write down your address or any account details.”

Ask for verbal consent to take anonymous notes. If consent is not given, end the session.

## Four tasks (12 minutes)

Give one task at a time without explaining the interface.

1. “Imagine sewage has backed up into your basement. Show what you would do first.”
2. “DWSD gave you a service-request number. Show where you would keep it and what you would expect next.”
3. “Show what the site can confirm and what still needs DWSD or a plumber.”
4. “Find whether there is a City program you could ask about, and its official next step.”

Record task completion as `unassisted`, `completed after prompt`, or `not completed`; record the screen or wording that caused difficulty. Do not write personal facts.

## Interview (10 minutes)

- What felt clear? What felt uncertain or misleading?
- Did any wording make it sound as if the site had contacted an agency or decided responsibility?
- What would you need before trusting this during an actual backup?
- Was anything hard to read or use on your phone?
- What language or accessibility support would make this useful to people you know?

## Close (3 minutes)

Restate that the product is early and that their feedback will be anonymized. Offer the official City/DWSD contacts already linked in the product if they have a real issue. Do not promise follow-up or assistance beyond what the partner has agreed to provide.
