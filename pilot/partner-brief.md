# Request to co-run a small BelowTrace resident pilot

BelowTrace is a free, open-source Detroit web tool for residents dealing with a basement sewage backup or a suspected private sewer-line problem. It brings together published City/DWSD program information and public records for one address, while clearly separating recorded facts from items that only DWSD, a licensed plumber, or a program can confirm.

We are asking for a community partner to co-run an 8–12 participant usability pilot. This is not a request to access client records, endorse the product, make a referral, or share resident contact information. The partner may recruit from its own community, choose sample addresses, observe every session, and stop the work at any time.

Each session is 30 minutes. Participants try four ordinary tasks: find the immediate reporting step, keep a service-request number, understand what requires confirmation, and locate a program's official application route. We will collect only anonymized task observations and feedback. We will not collect names, addresses, photos, account numbers, claims, or case documents.

The result will be a short findings memo shared with the partner before any public use. We will state the small sample and its limits; the pilot will not be described as proof of citywide outcomes.

Live product: https://belowtrace.vercel.app

Data and method: https://belowtrace.vercel.app/sources
Source code: https://github.com/ReffWu/belowtrace

## What we need

- One staff member or community lead to advise on wording and recruit 8–12 voluntary participants.
- A 45-minute setup conversation and permission to run the sessions remotely or at a community location.
- Feedback on the findings memo.

## What BelowTrace provides

- A facilitator, written session guide, consent wording, and anonymized findings memo.
- A product build that keeps case data in the participant's own browser.
- A commitment to fix material misunderstandings before expanding the pilot.
