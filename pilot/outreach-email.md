# Initial partner outreach — ready to send

## First choice: Detroit Housing Network

**To:** `DHN@CHNHousingPartners.org`
**Subject:** Request to co-run a small resident usability pilot for basement-backup navigation

Hello Detroit Housing Network team,

I’m Reff Wu, the builder of BelowTrace, a free open-source Detroit web tool that helps residents navigate the first steps after a basement sewage backup or suspected private sewer-line problem. It uses published City/DWSD program information and public records, but it explicitly does not diagnose a pipe, determine responsibility, submit a report, or make an eligibility decision.

I’m looking for one community partner to co-run an 8–12 participant, 30-minute usability pilot. This would not require client records, referrals, endorsement, or sharing resident contact information. The partner would control recruitment, may observe every session, and can stop the pilot at any time. We would use sample addresses by default and collect only anonymous task observations — never addresses, account numbers, photos, claims, or case documents.

The goal is to learn whether residents can find the immediate safe reporting step, keep a service-request number, understand what still needs DWSD or a plumber, and reach an official program application route. I would share the anonymized findings memo with your team before any public use.

Could we schedule a 30-minute conversation to see whether this is useful for your network or one of your member organizations?

Data and method: https://belowtrace.vercel.app/sources
Source code: https://github.com/ReffWu/belowtrace

Thank you,
Reff Wu
