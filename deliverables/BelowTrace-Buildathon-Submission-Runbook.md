## Three-minute core pitch

**0:00–0:25 — problem**

“When sewage enters a Detroit basement, people have to act before they know whether the issue is a city sewer, a private line, insurance, or a repair program. Our City snapshot has 14,115 water-in-basement 311 requests since January 2023. Those are requests, not unique households, but they show a recurring decision problem.”

**0:25–0:50 — product**

“BelowTrace turns that moment into one truthful action record. It reads the public records for one address, separates facts from unknowns, then helps the resident record a real DWSD report and hand the result to the next person who can act.”

**0:50–2:05 — live demo**

“Here is 16776 Prevost Street. I start a backup case. The tool asks the resident to call DWSD, keep the service-request number, and record only what they observed. If I choose ‘I tried, but did not reach them,’ the case stays at step one. It does not pretend DWSD is coming. Then the case file keeps the date, the service-request number, evidence checklist, sources, and the next verified question. A second address starts a separate case, so records do not mix.”

**2:05–2:35 — Detroit impact**

“This is for Detroit households facing a basement-water report, beginning where program routes are unclear. In our snapshot, 5,053 relevant requests came from outside PSRP neighborhoods. BelowTrace does not decide responsibility. It gives residents a source-backed next action before they spend money.”

**2:35–3:00 — ask**

“The resident tool stays free. We are asking for one Detroit neighborhood or housing-service partner, one sewer or housing expert, and 8–12 voluntary residents to test whether the record actually improves comprehension and follow-through. The site is live today.”
